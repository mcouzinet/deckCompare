Deck Compare — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Overview

Deck Compare is a browser extension for Chrome, Edge and Firefox that compares Magic: The Gathering decklists. It runs entirely in your browser: there is no account, no server of ours, and nothing about you is collected.

Data we collect

None. The developer receives no data from the extension: no personal information, no browsing history, no usage statistics.

What the extension reads

  • Decklists (card names, quantities and the deck's name) from the deck pages you open or the deck links you give it, on the supported sites: Moxfield, Archidekt, MTGGoldfish, mtgtop8, Magic-Ville, mtgdecks, Melee and getpaird; and from decklists you paste yourself.
  • The address of the tab you are on, when you click the extension's icon, to recognise a supported deck page.
  • Your public decks, if you enter your username for Moxfield, Archidekt or Magic-Ville: the list of that user's public decks on that site.

The extension only reads decks. It never posts, edits or changes anything on these sites.

Network requests

The extension contacts only the following:

  • The supported deck sites, to fetch the decks you chose to compare. Requests to MTGGoldfish, mtgdecks and Magic-Ville carry the cookies your browser already holds for those sites, so that their bot protection lets them through; the extension never reads or stores those cookies. When one of these sites blocks a direct request, the extension opens the deck page in a background tab, reads it and closes it.
  • Scryfall (api.scryfall.com, cards.scryfall.io), to get card types and images: the names of the cards on screen are sent to Scryfall for that purpose. See Scryfall's API terms.
  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com), to load the typefaces of the extension's own pages. See Google's privacy policy.

Stored on your device

The extension keeps the following in the browser's extension storage (storage.local), on your device only:

  • the two decks of the comparison in progress, so the results page can show them;
  • the decks you add to a cross-comparison, and the card filters you set on it;
  • your settings: whether the Compare button shows on deck sites, the usernames you entered and the public deck lists fetched for them, and the results page's display density;
  • a cache of card data from Scryfall (card types and image addresses), kept for 30 days so pages load faster.

Nothing is synced across devices or sent anywhere. Uninstalling the extension deletes all of it.

Permissions

  • activeTab: read the address and the decklist of the tab you are on, when you click the extension's icon.
  • storage: keep the data listed above on your device.
  • scripting: show the Compare button on the optional sites you allow.
  • Access to the supported deck sites and Scryfall: read decklists and card data, and show the Compare button on deck pages. On Firefox, you grant this access from the extension's popup the first time.
  • Optional access to Moxfield's pages and to the www / non-www variants of the supported sites: requested only when you turn on the Compare button, and removed when you turn it off.

Analytics and tracking

None. No analytics, no telemetry, no advertising, and the extension sets no cookies of its own.

Changes to this policy

Changes are published on this page, with a new date at the top.
