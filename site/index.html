DeckCompare

Compare any two MTG decklists, right on the deck page.

Shared cards, exclusives, quantity gaps and a similarity score, side by side. Works across nine deck sites, Moxfield, Archidekt and ManaBox among them. Free, no account, no personal data.

Chrome Web Store Edge Add-ons Firefox Add-ons Safari · soon

Brave, Opera, Vivaldi and Arc install it from the Chrome Web Store.

What you get

  • No copy-paste. The extension reads the deck from the page you are on, so a Moxfield list compares to an mtgtop8 one in one flow.
  • Every figure follows your filter. Commanders, mainboard or sideboard: the score and the counts are recomputed for what you look at.
  • Private by design. No account and no personal data: everything runs in your browser, apart from an anonymous usage count you can switch off.
